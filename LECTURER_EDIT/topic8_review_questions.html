Topic 8 Review Questions

Use these questions for revision, tutorial discussion and self-study. Attempt each question before opening the suggested answer.

A. Assistive technologies

1. What is an assistive technology?
Suggested answer
A product or system that helps a person perform activities that may otherwise be difficult or impossible.
2. Give two examples of AI supporting communication accessibility.
Suggested answer
Speech-to-text captions, text-to-speech and augmentative or alternative communication tools are examples.
3. Give two examples of AI supporting people with visual impairments.
Suggested answer
Image description, object recognition, text recognition and navigation support are examples.
4. Why should assistive AI be designed with users rather than only for users?
Suggested answer
Because accessibility needs and preferences vary, and intended users can identify barriers that designers may otherwise miss.

B. Personalised learning

5. What does personalised learning mean in this topic?
Suggested answer
Adapting learning materials, pace, difficulty, feedback or interaction format to better support an individual learner's needs.
6. Give three ways a learning system can adapt content for a student with disabilities.
Suggested answer
Examples include text/audio alternatives, simplified language, adjustable pacing, extra practice, accessible input methods and highlighted key information.
7. Why should personalised learning not replace teacher judgement?
Suggested answer
Automated systems can misunderstand context or learner needs, while teachers can interpret broader educational and personal factors.
8. What privacy concern can arise in personalised learning?
Suggested answer
The system may collect sensitive information about disability, performance or behaviour, so collection and use should be limited and protected.

C. Mobility and accessibility

9. Why is the shortest route not always the most accessible route?
Suggested answer
The shortest route may contain stairs, inaccessible surfaces, broken lifts or other barriers.
10. What data could an accessibility-aware route planner use?
Suggested answer
Locations of ramps, lifts, accessible entrances, surface conditions, transport status and user preferences.
11. Why does up-to-date local information matter for mobility AI?
Suggested answer
An outdated route can become unusable or unsafe if an accessible facility is closed, broken or incorrectly mapped.
12. Why should users be able to specify route preferences?
Suggested answer
Different people have different mobility needs, so accessibility cannot be defined by one fixed route criterion.

D. Mental health and emotional well-being

13. Give two appropriate supportive uses of AI for emotional well-being.
Suggested answer
General check-ins, journalling prompts, healthy-routine reminders and links to human support services are examples.
14. Why should AI not be treated as a substitute for qualified mental-health care?
Suggested answer
Clinical assessment and treatment require professional judgement, context and responsibility that a general AI system cannot safely replace.
15. What should a well-being system do when a situation exceeds its safe scope?
Suggested answer
It should clearly communicate its limitation and direct or escalate the user to appropriate qualified human support.
16. Why is privacy especially important in mental-health applications?
Suggested answer
Mental-health information is highly sensitive and misuse or disclosure can cause personal and social harm.

E. Inclusive design

17. What does it mean to provide alternative interaction modes?
Suggested answer
Users should not depend on only one method such as visual text, speech or touch; alternatives such as text, audio, keyboard or voice can reduce barriers.
18. Why should accessibility be evaluated rather than assumed?
Suggested answer
A system that appears accessible to designers may still fail for actual users, devices, environments or disability contexts.
19. Why does user control matter in assistive AI?
Suggested answer
Users should be able to accept, reject or modify support because they understand their own needs and context better than an automated system.
20. What is the central principle connecting all four curriculum areas in Topic 8?
Suggested answer
AI should expand access, autonomy and participation while protecting users from avoidable harm and preserving appropriate human support.
