## About the Course

DT 111 introduces the fundamental ideas, methods and applications of **Data Science** and **Artificial Intelligence**. The course is designed to help students build a clear foundation before progressing to more specialised topics in data analysis, machine learning and AI.

The learning materials combine explanations, examples, interactive demonstrations, review questions, quizzes and practical assignments.

## Course Topics

The course is organised into eight topics:

1. **Introduction to Data Science and AI**  
   Overview, history, evolution, applications and impact.

2. **Data Collection and Preprocessing**  
   Data sources, data quality, cleaning, missing values, transformation and preparation.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis**  
   Summary statistics, distributions, visualisation, patterns and interpretation.

4. **Statistical Methods for Data Analysis**  
   Probability, hypothesis testing, relationships and introductory regression.

5. **Introduction to Machine Learning**  
   Supervised learning, unsupervised learning, model training and evaluation.

6. **Artificial Intelligence Basics**  
   Search, reasoning, rule-based systems and introductory neural-network concepts.

7. **Ethical Implications and Societal Impact**  
   Privacy, bias, fairness, accountability and responsible AI.

8. **Contemporary Issues in AI**  
   Emerging applications and the use of AI in areas such as accessibility, learning, mobility and well-being.

## Learning Approach

The course is designed for active learning. Students are encouraged to:

- follow the examples rather than only reading definitions;
- interact with the demonstrations and explain what they observe;
- attempt questions before revealing suggested answers;
- connect Data Science and AI concepts to real problems;
- complete practical activities independently;
- ask questions whenever a concept or result is unclear;
- consider ethical and responsible use of data and AI throughout the course.
