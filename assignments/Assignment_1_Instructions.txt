DT 111 — Assignment 1
Data Science and AI Around Us

Purpose
Identify a real problem in your community, institution or sector that could benefit from Data Science or AI, and explain how data might contribute to understanding or addressing the problem.

Task
1. Choose one real-world problem.
2. Describe the problem clearly in approximately 150–200 words.
3. Identify at least three types of data that could be useful.
4. State where those data might come from.
5. Explain whether the problem mainly requires descriptive analysis, prediction, an AI capability, or a combination.
6. Identify one potential benefit and one ethical or practical risk.
7. Draw a simple workflow from problem → data → analysis/model → output → decision.
