DT 111 — Assignment 2
Data Quality Audit and Cleaning

Purpose
Apply Topic 2 concepts by inspecting, documenting and cleaning a small dataset.

Files
- datasets/topic2_messy_student_data.csv
- datasets/topic2_cleaned_student_data_example.csv (for comparison after attempting the task)

Task
1. Open the messy dataset in a spreadsheet application.
2. Identify at least eight data-quality issues.
3. Create a cleaning log with columns: problem, affected field/row, action taken, reason.
4. Create your own cleaned version. Do not overwrite the original raw file.
5. Write 200–300 words explaining two cleaning decisions where more than one reasonable option was possible.
6. Submit the cleaned dataset and your report/cleaning log.

Submission
Upload to the DT 111 Google Drive assignment folder configured in LECTURER_EDIT/course_settings.js.

Recommended filenames
DT111_A2_RegistrationNumber.xlsx (or .csv)
DT111_A2_RegistrationNumber_Report.pdf
