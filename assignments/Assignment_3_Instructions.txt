DT 111 — Assignment 3
Exploratory Data Analysis Report

Purpose
Use descriptive statistics and visualisation to explore a clean dataset and communicate evidence-based observations.

Dataset
datasets/topic3_student_learning_data.csv

Task
1. Describe the dataset: number of observations, variables and data types.
2. Calculate suitable descriptive statistics for final_score, attendance_pct and study_hours_week.
3. Create at least three appropriate visualisations:
   - one distribution chart;
   - one group-comparison chart;
   - one relationship chart.
4. For each chart, write 2–4 sentences explaining what it shows.
5. Identify at least three patterns or insights.
6. State one question that would require further investigation.
7. Include a short paragraph explaining why an observed relationship should not automatically be interpreted as causal.

Expected submission
A concise 3–5 page EDA report containing summary statistics, charts, interpretation and a short conclusion.
