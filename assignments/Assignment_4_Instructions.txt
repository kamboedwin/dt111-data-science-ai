DT 111 — Assignment 4
Introductory Statistical Interpretation Task

Purpose
Develop conceptual understanding of probability, hypothesis testing and simple regression interpretation without overloading calculation.

Dataset
datasets/topic4_regression_learning_data.csv

Task
1. Explain the difference between a discrete and a continuous variable, giving one example of each.
2. Write one null hypothesis and one alternative hypothesis for the question:
   Does a new teaching intervention change students’ average score?
3. Explain what a p-value of 0.02 would suggest at the 0.05 significance level.
4. Explain why this does not prove the alternative hypothesis with absolute certainty.
5. Using the supplied dataset, create a scatter plot of study_hours_week against final_score.
6. Add a trend line if your spreadsheet tool allows it, or describe the relationship visually.
7. Write 200–300 words interpreting the relationship and explaining why it should not automatically be interpreted as causal.

Expected submission
A short report containing written answers and the scatter plot.
