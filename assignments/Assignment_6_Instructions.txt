DT 111 — Assignment 6
AI Problem-Solving Approaches

Purpose
Compare different Artificial Intelligence approaches and justify which technique is suitable for a problem.

Dataset
datasets/topic6_ai_problem_scenarios.csv

Task
1. For each supplied scenario, identify the most suitable broad approach:
   - search/planning;
   - logic/reasoning;
   - supervised Machine Learning;
   - clustering;
   - neural-network-based perception/language.
2. Choose any three scenarios and justify your choices in 2–4 sentences each.
3. Use the Search Explorer with BFS, DFS and A*. Record:
   - number of states visited;
   - path length;
   - one observation about how the algorithms explored differently.
4. Use the Rule-Based Reasoning Explorer. Change at least two facts and record which conclusions are or are not inferred.
5. Use the Neural-Network Activation Explorer with at least three input combinations and explain what changes in the hidden/output activations illustrate.
6. Write a short conclusion explaining why AI should be viewed as a toolbox rather than as a single algorithm.

Important
The interactive neural network is an educational illustration, not a real trained student-performance model. Its output must not be interpreted as an actual probability or decision about a student.
