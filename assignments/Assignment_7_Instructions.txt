DT 111 — Assignment 7
Responsible AI Case Review

Purpose
Analyse an AI or Data Science scenario from both technical and ethical perspectives.

Dataset
datasets/topic7_responsible_ai_cases.csv

Task
1. Choose one case from the supplied scenario file.
2. Describe the intended purpose of the system and identify at least three affected stakeholders.
3. Identify the main ethical risks. Consider privacy, bias, fairness, transparency, accountability, human oversight and wider societal effects where relevant.
4. Identify what additional evidence you would need before deciding whether the system should be deployed.
5. Propose at least five practical safeguards or design changes.
6. Explain how the system should be monitored after deployment and what should trigger review, correction or suspension.
7. Conclude whether the system should be:
   - deployed as described;
   - deployed only after changes; or
   - not deployed.
   Justify your position.

Expected submission
A concise 3–5 page case review. Support conclusions with clear reasoning rather than merely listing ethical keywords.
